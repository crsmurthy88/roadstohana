Executive roadmap

Three roads lead to HANA.

The technology route matters because it changes the Finance design, data, controls, testing burden and operating model. Choose the road deliberately.

Greenfield

Build new on standard

Use when the legacy design is itself the problem. Rebuild the operating model, chart of accounts, controls and processes around the future state.

GROW / Fit-to-Standard
Brownfield

Convert in place

Preserve history and configuration where it still serves the business. The assessment matters more than the migration because old compromises come with it.

RISE / Conversion
Bluefield

Selective transition

Keep what works and rebuild what does not. Often the practical answer for large landscapes that need both continuity and redesign.

Hybrid / Selective
