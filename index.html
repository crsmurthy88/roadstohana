Transition approaches

Three ways to get there. The choice is the first deliverable.

Road 1 · Greenfield

Start clean

New implementation on standard. The right call when the legacy design is itself the problem, when the business model has changed, or when reporting and regulatory demands have outgrown what the old configuration can carry.

Road 2 · Brownfield

Convert in place

System conversion that preserves history and configuration. Faster and cheaper where the existing design still holds — but it carries every old compromise forward, so the assessment matters more than the migration.

Road 3 · Bluefield

Selective transition

Convert what works, rebuild what doesn't. Usually the honest answer for large landscapes: a clean chart of accounts and new ledger design alongside retained transactional history and proven process areas.
